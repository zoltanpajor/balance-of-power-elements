Elements

Board

Drag a field element onto the board to place it. Drag a placed field element to move it. Drag it off the board to remove it.

ℹ️
  • Each element can be placed up to 6 times on the board.
  • The center hex can't hold a field element.
  • Ring 1 (the 6 hexes touching the center) can hold at most one of each element.
  • No two neighboring hexes can hold the same element.
  • Ring 1 must be completely filled before you can place further out.
  • Beyond ring 1, a new field element must neighbor at least 2 existing field elements.

Instead of simulating board setup - you are generating an automated board setup!
